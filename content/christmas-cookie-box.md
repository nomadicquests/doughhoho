# Christmas Cookie Box campaign

**Goal:** make Dough Ho Ho Bakery top of mind this Christmas and take preorders
for the Christmas Cookie Box.

**Where:** Instagram feed, Reels and TikTok, plus paid ads on Meta (Instagram
and Facebook).

**Feel:** fun Christmas meets classic Christmas. Classic comes from the gingham,
rosemary, ribbon, serif headings, and the green and gold. Fun comes from cheeky
copy, playful motion, and treating each cookie like a character.

## The offer

**Christmas Cookie Box: J$2,400**

12 cookies + a free Peppermint Snow Ball: a baker's dozen.

| Cookie | Per box |
| ------ | ------- |
| Candy Cane | 4 |
| Butter Cookies | 4 |
| Cranberry Pistachio | 2 |
| Triple Ginger Snap | 2 |
| Peppermint Snow Balls | 1, free |

- **Order:** through the order form or on WhatsApp.
- **Last day to order:** Friday 18 December.
- **Businesses:** order boxes for your team or your Secret Santa. WhatsApp us
  for details.

## The challenge

Cookie boxes aren't a Jamaican Christmas tradition yet, so the content has to
show what the box is and where it fits in a Jamaican Christmas, not only sell
it.

## Lines to use

- *A baker's dozen of Christmas.* This is the main line: it explains the box
  and the freebie.
- *Black cake has company.*
- *Sorrel's new best friend.*
- *Who's on your list?*
- *Secret Santa, sorted.* (for businesses)

## Rules for visuals

- Anything about **the box** shows only the five cookies above.
- Tray shots with the full set, pinwheels included, are fine as background,
  filler and "baking day" footage.
- No one appears on camera. Everything is built from existing photos, the
  cookie cut-outs and existing clips, with motion graphics.

## Three phases

### 1. Meet the box: 16 to 22 November
Fill the grid before any ads run. A new page with nothing on it puts off people
who click through from an ad.

### 2. Preorders open: 23 November to 13 December
Ads run. Rotate between the box itself, one cookie at a time, gifting, and
Secret Santa for businesses.

### 3. Last call: 14 to 18 December
Countdown posts to the 18 December deadline.

The dated posts are in [`calendar.md`](calendar.md).

## Ads

The budget is $100 for the cookie box and the Christmas cakes together. This
plan assumes US dollars; confirm.

- **Cookie box: about $60.** Christmas cakes get the other $40 once that
  campaign is planned.
- **Use one ad at a time** so the small budget isn't split.
  1. **23 Nov to 6 Dec, about $30:** the "Preorders open" Reel.
  2. **7 to 18 Dec, about $30:** boost whichever post has done best by then,
     with a last-call caption.
- **Goal: messages, sending people to WhatsApp.** Orders come in on WhatsApp,
  so a "Send WhatsApp message" button goes straight to an order. This needs
  WhatsApp Business connected to the Facebook/Instagram page.
- **Targeting:** only the areas you deliver to, adults 22 to 55, and broad
  interests. Let Meta find the buyers.
- **Businesses are reached without ads:** a Secret Santa post pinned to the
  profile, plus sharing that post directly with companies you know on WhatsApp.

## What to build

These are templates in `studio/`, in the order needed:

1. **Meet the Cookies:** a Reel and a carousel. Each cut-out cookie pops in with
   its name and how many are in the box, ending on "+ a free Peppermint Snow
   Ball".
2. **Box Reveal:** a Reel. The cookies drop into the box one by one, then the
   price and "Order now".
3. **Cookie Spotlight:** one cookie, big, with a one-line description. Works as
   a still and as a short Reel.
4. **Announcement:** a still and a Story for posts that are mostly text, such as
   "Preorders open", "Secret Santa, sorted" and the countdowns.
